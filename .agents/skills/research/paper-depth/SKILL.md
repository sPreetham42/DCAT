# Paper Depth

Ensure the final manuscript reaches 15+ substantive pages.
Substantive expansion may come from technical explanation, equations, related work, dataset/leakage methodology, experimental protocol, baseline details, ablation/statistical/efficiency/error analysis, discussion, and limitations.

**CRITICAL:** Never inflate the paper through meaningless repetition.

# Paper Author

Write an advanced research manuscript.
Requirements:
* Academic quality and technical depth
* Strong research narrative with detailed methodology
* Mathematical formulation where appropriate
* Detailed experimental methodology, baseline analysis, and ablations
* Statistical, efficiency, and error analysis
* Discussion and limitations
* Evidence-backed conclusions

**Target:** 15+ pages. (Preferred: 16-20 pages).

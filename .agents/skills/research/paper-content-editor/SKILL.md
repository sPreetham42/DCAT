# Paper Content Editor

Rules for `paper/PAPER_CONTENT.md`:
* This is the editable master content.
* Human corrections must be preserved.
* Evidence-backed content must not be silently replaced.
* Claims must remain traceable. Table/figure source mappings must be preserved.
* Uncertainty must be marked clearly (e.g. `[VERIFY...]`).
